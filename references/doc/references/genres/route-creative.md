# Genre Contract: Creative / Narrative Creation (`router.creative`)

<a id="体裁规则表硬约束"></a>
## Genre Rules Table (Hard Constraints)

| Rule Item | Rule |
|-|-|
| Writing Style | Language, rhythm, and point of view serve the specified narrative experience; expressive freedom does not replace character motivation, causality, and scene readability |
| Content Logic | This contract covers only narrative creation; first confirm the experience, length, point of view, and constraints, and use "character desire → resistance → choice → cost → change" to form scene causality; scripts and interactive narratives respectively obey performable action and consequential branching |
| Facts / Boundaries | User-authorized fiction may be invented, but real backgrounds, citations, established canon of existing works, and character identities must not be fabricated; images, epigraphs, excerpts from original works, and creative references must comply with source and usage permissions, and non-text references and interactive branch diagrams must include textual equivalents of relationships, paths, and states; distinguish between explicit settings, reasonable creative filling-in, and constraints pending confirmation; when core worldview / rights boundaries conflict and cannot be safely narrowed, blocked |
| Errors | Merely piling up settings without choices occurring, characters suddenly losing motivation to advance the plot, conflict resolved by coincidence or external force without cost, unintentional drift in point of view / tense, scripts written as explanatory novels, interactive branches with no state differences, or silently including poetry in the deliverable—any one of these constitutes failure |

<a id="适用与消歧"></a>
## Applicability and Disambiguation

Only used for creation centered on events, character choices, and change, such as web fiction, short stories, fan narratives, interactive fiction, scripts, and story outlines. Poetry, lyrics, and purely lyrical prose are outside the scope of this contract; when such requests are received, first confirm the goal or use the corresponding dedicated rules, and do not silently expand the scope merely because of the top-level name "Creative."

Expressing judgments through arguments and evidence goes to Opinion; building professional credibility through real personal experience goes to Personal Brand. If the goal of a worldview explanation is merely knowledge explanation, it does not become a story just because it includes character names.

<a id="子类型"></a>
## Subtypes

| Subtype | Reader Task and Progression |
|-|-|
| Short Story / Web Fiction | Obtain a continuous narrative experience; trigger change → escalate resistance → key choice → consequence / echo |
| Fan Narrative | Experience new situations on the agreed canon and character core; clarify the time point / divergence point → character choice → new consequences |
| Script / Short Drama / Narrative Short Video | See filmable, performable action and conflict; medium / duration / production constraints → scene goal → visuals, action, sound / dialogue → turning point → scene state change |
| Interactive Fiction | Make choices based on information and with consequences; state → choice → feedback → state change → subsequent branches |
| Story Outline | Judge whether the story can hold up and continue to be created; premise → character arc → node causality → climax choice → ending change |

<a id="设定与真实性"></a>
## Settings and Authenticity

- First lock in the characters, relationships, forbidden zones, canon time points, and desired experience given by the user; unspecified creative space may be filled in, but explicit constraints must not be overridden. When key ambiguities have multiple directions that would significantly change the finished product, ask first.
- When using real places, history, science, or cultural materials, verify facts that would affect the plot; intentional alternate settings should let readers recognize their fictional conventions. Fan creation must not pass off original settings as canon, nor fabricate quotations from the original work.
- Real people, undisclosed experiences, protected materials, and commissioned works are handled according to authorization boundaries; when usability cannot be confirmed, switch to an original substitute or remain blocked.

<a id="结构与高质量写法"></a>
## Structure and High-Quality Writing

Every scene has characters take action toward a goal and changes information, relationships, resources, or risk by the time they leave. Details simultaneously serve sensory, character, or foreshadowing functions, and background is released through current conflict rather than explained in concentrated blocks. Dialogue must change the situation rather than repeat narration; script action, sound, and staging must be achievable under the declared conditions of actors, locations, props, and medium, and fixed formulas such as "first three seconds / one reversal per minute" must not replace causality. The ending fulfills the choices and costs established earlier. Outlines may explicitly present structure, while finished drafts transform structure into experienceable scenes.
