# Genre Router: Report (`router.report`)

Use this category when forming insights from data, samples, or research; choose according to the reader's task and read only one leaf; keywords are used only for recall, and `报告 / 分析 / 研究 / 数据 / 白皮书` alone does not determine routing. Organizational execution / approval goes to Workplace, and self-directed learning goes to Knowledge.

| Reader task / keywords, strong signals, and exclusions | Leaf |
|-|-|
| Answer a well-defined research question, where methods, samples, and generalizable boundaries determine credibility; research reports, interviews / questionnaires / user research. Exclude when only interpreting established metrics | [`research-report.md`](research-report.md) |
| Interpret defined metrics, trends, distributions, funnels, or experimental observations; data reports, business data, metric reviews. Exclude when samples need to be redesigned to answer the question | [`data-report.md`](data-report.md) |
| Enable professional readers to systematically understand and evaluate a problem, framework, or solution; white papers, industry frameworks, technical / policy topics. Exclude when lead generation, product selling points, or CTAs dominate | [`white-paper.md`](white-paper.md) |
| Compare strategic, investment, market, product, or resource options and their costs, benefits, and risks; business analysis, feasibility, entry / build-or-buy decisions. Exclude when the body requires a named decision-maker to choose / approve, or serves as an entry point for authorization, resource allocation, or execution commitment | [`business-analysis.md`](business-analysis.md) |
