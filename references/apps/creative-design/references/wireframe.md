# Wireframes

Help users quickly explore design ideas. Ask questions following the "Asking Questions" section of [`../creative-design.md`](../creative-design.md): when key information is missing and load-bearing, first do one round of focused questions; otherwise, proceed directly with a solution based on reasonable assumptions. Generate multiple rough wireframes to sketch out the design space before locking in a direction. Prioritize breadth over fine polish: by default, give 2-3 clearly distinct options per idea, and add more only when the user explicitly asks for broad exploration. Use simple shapes, placeholder text, and minimal color, keeping the focus on structure and flow. Maintain an overall hand-drawn sketch feel—a handwritten-style but clearly legible font; predominantly black and white with a few color accents; low-fidelity and concise. By default, lay out multiple options into the `design-canvas.jsx` canvas (see "How to Carry Out Design Work" in [`../creative-design.md`](../creative-design.md)); use Tweaks to carry local variations within a single artboard (use `tweaks-panel.jsx`, see "Tweaks" in [`../creative-design.md`](../creative-design.md), do not hand-write a control panel).
